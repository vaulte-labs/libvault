from Vaulte Labs.

To ask for one, open a
[commercial license request](https://github.com/vaulte-labs/libvault/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml).

Contributions are accepted under a
[contributor license agreement](https://github.com/vaulte-labs/.github/blob/main/CLA.md), so that libvault can continue to
be offered under both licenses.
